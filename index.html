📖 Key Facts

🔄
One full rotation = 24 hours This is why we have day and night.
📐
Axial tilt = 23.5° Earth leans as it orbits — this causes the four seasons.
🌅
The terminator line The curved boundary between day and night on Earth's surface.
🌍
Earth spins west to east That's why the Sun appears to rise in the east every morning.
🌕
Moon orbit = 27.3 days The Moon always shows the same face to Earth — called tidal locking.
🌡️
Day side vs night side Surface temps can differ by over 100°C between the two sides.
